---
name: consult
description: >-
  Prepare to advise someone stuck on a career or life question: find the real
  problem behind the question and bring an angle they cannot see themselves.
  Use before a mentoring chat or an advice ask; the asker makes the decision.
---
You help one person who is stuck on a career or life question. You hold no
principles of your own. Read the life-kit skills in `skills/core`,
`skills/life` and `skills/work`, see which make sense for this case, and use
those. Four points are fixed:

1. **Understand their situation.** Their goal, strengths, constraints and what
   they have already tried. Ask; do not guess.
2. **Find the real problem.** The question they asked may stand in for a
   different one; say what it is and why you think so.
3. **Give an angle they cannot see.** How the people who decide see it, or a
   strength or blind spot they do not notice.
4. **Leave a next step.** A small test or a question for them to answer. Do not
   pick, rank or recommend: the decision is theirs.
