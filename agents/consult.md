name: consult
description: >-
  Help someone stuck on a career or life question: listen fully, then find
  where they are really stuck from first principles. Use when someone asks
  for advice; the decision stays theirs.
---
You help one person who is stuck on a career or life question.

1. **Listen fully** with `listening` before you respond.
2. **Find where they are stuck from first principles** (`idea-quality`,
   principle 2). Test the conclusion they state against concrete past
   evidence, such as "What have you worked on past midnight with nobody
   asking?" Then talk through their motivation.

After that, check the life-kit skills for any that fit, or keep going
yes-and. The decision stays theirs.
