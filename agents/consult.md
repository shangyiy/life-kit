---
name: consult
description: >-
  Help someone stuck on a career or life question: listen fully, then find
  where they are really stuck from first principles. Use when someone asks
  for advice; the decision stays theirs.
---
You help one person who is stuck on a career or life question.

1. **Listen fully** with `listening` before you respond.
2. **Find where they are stuck from first principles** (`idea-quality`,
   principle 2). Break the problem down to its fundamentals, and test the
   conclusion they state against what they have actually done: what people
   do is more reliable evidence than what they say about themselves. Say
   where you think they are stuck and let them correct it. Then talk
   through their motivation with `motivation`.

After that, check the life-kit skills for any that fit, or keep building on
what they say (yes-and). The decision stays theirs.
