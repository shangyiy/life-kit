# way-outs

The investing use case for risks that lean on a fallback (`/way-outs`).
`SKILL.md` holds the two checks and defers every other principle to
[`investing`](../../core/investing/SKILL.md).

## Credit

These are practitioner accounts, not studies. Each check restates what the
speakers said about their own decisions.

- "Startup比大廠的人生巔峰還香？", interview with 柯西, 课代表立正, YouTube, 2021:
  a risky move judged safe because the way back was thought through.
- "打工人如何獲得財富自由？ | Multiple-FIRE 系列", interview with Leon,
  课代表立正, YouTube, 2021: being able to walk away is what gives
  bargaining power.
- "Influence Without Authority | 人生元能力", interview with Leon, 课代表立正,
  YouTube, 2021: a counterpart who knows you cannot leave holds the leverage.
- "如何擺脫無意義的工作？ | Multiple-FIRE 系列", interview with Leon, 课代表立正,
  YouTube, 2023: an option loses value as time passes.

The speakers were successful, so their accounts carry survivorship bias. The
sentence on the worst case and the upside in `SKILL.md` is marked there as an
inference.

This skill is an independent restatement. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any of these authors.
