# Region: San Francisco Bay Area and the Central Coast

Use when the packet location is on the San Francisco Peninsula, in the South Bay, or within the getaway limit of Santa Cruz and the coast. Search in English. These are hints, not facts; verify every quest live.

## Sources

- Weather: `api.weather.gov` or `weather.gov` for the town, the coast and the destination.
- State parks: `parks.ca.gov` for hours, closures, reservations and fees. Guided walks book through ReserveCalifornia.
- National parks: `nps.gov`.
- Cities and counties: the city page, `smcgov.org` (San Mateo County, including its certified farmers market list), and visitor bureaus such as `santacruz.org`.
- Open space and park districts: `openspace.org` for guided hikes (many close registration a day before).
- Markets: a city page, the county list, or the market operator's own page. Markets here are weekly farmers markets, flea markets and holiday pop-ups.

## Quest ideas

- Highway 1 coastal parks form a scenic-route cluster from Half Moon Bay down to Santa Cruz. One cluster can fill a side quest or an expedition, with the stops checked one by one.
- Natural Bridges State Beach (Santa Cruz) for the winter monarch preserve, and Año Nuevo State Park for elephant seals, are seasonal quests. Their seasons shift year to year and their access rules change by month, so confirm the target date on the park page.
- Other seasonal quests: whale migration from coastal headlands, wildflowers in spring, fall color in the hills. Offer one only when an official or current source confirms it for the date, as the Seasonal sight rule in SKILL.md says.
- Dated events on Halloween and Día de los Muertos weekends, at cemeteries, parks, museums and plazas.
