# Region: Japan

Use when the packet location is in Japan. Search in Japanese.

- Events: イベント, 祭り, 区民行事.
- Markets: 朝市, 日曜マルシェ. A 直売所 or 産直 (farm-stand or JA market) and a 卸売 market open to the public count as markets in the Ranking.
- Official sources: `.go.jp` and `.lg.jp` city or ward pages, and JA pages.
