# next-goal

Find the next goals worth pursuing from the person's own motive and hand a
short list of strong candidates back for them to choose (`/next-goal`).

## Credit

- Step 1's habit of asking why each answer matters until the reason is
  concrete resembles laddering, and draws on it by analogy: Thomas J. Reynolds
  and Jonathan Gutman, "Laddering Theory, Method, Analysis, and
  Interpretation," *Journal of Advertising Research* 28(1), 1988, pp. 11-31.
- Everything else is the author's own.
