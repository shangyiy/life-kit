# review-resume

Review a resume against a job description the way a hiring manager with a
30-second budget would (`/review-resume`): a fit verdict, blind spots, and a
reorder suggestion that puts the job's top signals where the reader sees them.
The reader's-side view follows `/perspective`.

## Credit

- Jakob Nielsen, "F-Shaped Pattern For Reading Web Content", Nielsen Norman
  Group, 2006 (readers scan the top and the left edge; the first words of a
  line get the most fixations)
- TheLadders, "Eye-Tracking Study", 2018 (recruiters scan a resume in seconds
  and spend the most time on job titles)
- The reader's-side framing comes from the credits of `perspective`.
