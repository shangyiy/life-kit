# optionality

Find the real choices you have and judge what each is worth
(`/optionality`).

## Credit

- Leon (guest), "Influence Without Authority ｜ 人生元能力", 课代表立正
  (YouTube), 2021-07-27, https://youtu.be/CTcMvIZFQcw: the freedom to walk
  away from a deal or offer.
- Leon (guest), "如何摆脱无意义的工作？｜Multiple-Fire系列", 课代表立正 (YouTube), 2023-05-23,
  https://youtu.be/dlv97OHFnGY: a choice such as taking time off loses worth as
  time passes and is near nothing once its window has closed.
- An anonymous contributor's unpublished short essay: an environment sets the
  chances, not the outcome.
- Hakan Erdogmus, course notes for CMU 18-657 (Decision Analysis and Engineering
  Economics for Software Engineers): the value of flexibility under uncertainty;
  a choice as a right, not a duty, whose loss has a floor while its gain stays
  open, and which becomes a duty once used; the cost of keeping a choice apart
  from what cannot be undone once it is used; the value of waiting for
  information; judging a choice by comparing the situation with and without it
  across bad, typical, and good outcomes, in words or numbers, with the worst
  case kept bearable; and the choices a path leaves after the decision: to wait,
  stop, expand, or switch.
- The other ideas are the author's own.
