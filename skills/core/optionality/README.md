# optionality

Find the real choices you have and judge what each is worth; it never
chooses for you (`/optionality`).

## Credit

- Leon (guest), "Influence Without Authority ｜ 人生元能力", 课代表立正
  (YouTube), 2021-07-27, https://youtu.be/CTcMvIZFQcw: the freedom to walk
  away from a deal or offer, and hearing no with less fear when you have a real
  alternative.
- Leon (guest), "如何摆脱无意义的工作？｜Multiple-Fire系列", 课代表立正 (YouTube), 2023-05-23,
  https://youtu.be/dlv97OHFnGY: a choice such as taking time off loses worth as
  time passes and is near nothing once its window has closed.
- An anonymous contributor's unpublished short essay: an environment sets the
  chances, not the outcome.
- Hakan Erdogmus, course notes for CMU 18-657 (Decision Analysis and
  Engineering Economics for Software Engineers): the value of flexibility
  under uncertainty; a choice as a right, not a duty, whose worst case is the
  cost of keeping it while its upside stays open; a choice counting only if
  you would act on it; waiting for information but acting before the window
  closes; and judging a path by the choices it leaves after the decision: to
  wait, stop, expand, switch, or sell off what is left.
- The other ideas are the author's own.
