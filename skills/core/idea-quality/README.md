# idea-quality

Principles for what makes an idea good, in the spirit of Christopher
Alexander's patterns (`/idea-quality`).

## Credit

- *Notes on the Synthesis of Form*, Christopher Alexander
- *A Pattern Language*, Christopher Alexander, Sara Ishikawa, Murray Silverstein
- "How to Get Startup Ideas", Paul Graham (paulgraham.com/startupideas.html)
- *Physics* I.1 and *Posterior Analytics* I.2, Aristotle
- Interview by Kevin Rose (*Foundation*, 2012), Elon Musk
- "Structured design" (IBM Systems Journal, 1974), W. Stevens, G. Myers, L. Constantine

This skill is an independent restatement. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any of these authors.
