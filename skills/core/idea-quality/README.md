# idea-quality

Principles for judging whether an idea is good by reading it
(`/idea-quality`): it solves a common, recurring problem; it is one simple,
cohesive idea that makes sense from first principles; and it grew from a real
need, as in Paul Graham's "organic" ideas.

## Credit

- Aristotle, *Physics* I.1, 184a10-16, and *Posterior Analytics* I.2,
  71b19-72a5
- Elon Musk, interview by Kevin Rose, *Foundation*, 8 September 2012 ("reason
  from first principles rather than by analogy"; batteries at $600/kWh
  against about $80 of raw materials)
- W. Stevens, G. Myers, L. Constantine, "Structured design", *IBM Systems
  Journal* 13(2), 115-139, 1974
- Paul Graham, "How to Get Startup Ideas", November 2012,
  paulgraham.com/startupideas.html
- Christopher Alexander, Sara Ishikawa, Murray Silverstein, *A Pattern
  Language*, Oxford University Press, 1977
