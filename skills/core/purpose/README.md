# purpose

Principles for keeping means, outputs, and metrics in service of a purpose
(`/purpose`).

## Credit

- Leon, “打工人如何获得财富自由？｜
  什么才是真正的财富和真正的自由？（中文字幕）｜
  Multiple-Fire系列,” guest on 課代表立正, 2021-08-06,
  https://youtu.be/9LKJ8JdLtfI: principles 1–4 on keeping ends separate from
  money, promotion, ratings, processes, and other means.
- Charles A. E. Goodhart, “Problems of Monetary Management: The U.K.
  Experience,” presented in 1975 and published in *Papers in Monetary
  Economics* 1, Reserve Bank of Australia, 1976, and Marilyn Strathern,
  “‘Improving ratings’: audit in the British University system,”
  *European Review* 5(3), 1997: principle 5 on a metric drifting from its
  purpose when it becomes a target.
- Donald T. Campbell, “Assessing the Impact of Planned Social Change,”
  *Evaluation and Program Planning* 2(1), 1979: principle 5 on pressure from
  decisions distorting an indicator.
- Steven Kerr, “On the Folly of Rewarding A, While Hoping for B,”
  *Academy of Management Journal* 18(4), 1975: principle 9 on rewarding conduct
  different from the conduct wanted.
- Jerry Z. Muller, *The Tyranny of Metrics*, Princeton University Press, 2018:
  principles 4–5 on metrics supporting judgment rather than replacing it.
- Owning the result, method changes, open purpose changes, trade-offs,
  boundaries, workflow, checklist, anti-patterns, and output are the author's
  own.
