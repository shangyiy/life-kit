# listening

Principles for deciding why you are listening and listening that way
(`/listening`). `SKILL.md` holds the definitions and principles.

## Credit

- "Influence Without Authority | 人生元能力" (interview with Leon, 课代表立正,
  YouTube, 2021): the four purposes of listening, the clash between empathy
  and evaluation, restating, and the timing of advice.

Principle 5's "ask first" is this skill's reading of the source, which says
only that the speaker is ready for advice after finishing.

This skill is an independent restatement. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any of these authors.
