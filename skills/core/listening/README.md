# listening

Principles for deciding why you listen, then listening, asking, and telling in
that order (`/listening`).

These ideas are the author's own reading:

- Three-level empathy, ending in siding with the change; siding needs no
  permission.
- Hear the whole account first; skipping it makes them feel you are not with them.
- The speaker's need decides where listening stops.
- Ask before guiding or giving a view; guide from the speaker's position.
- The direct-answer exceptions.
- Tell little, and the reason for it.
- The noise filter and the harm override.

There is no literature source. These principles are not affiliated with or
endorsed by anyone.
