# /listening

The domain of listening: taking in what another person says, in any setting,
whatever you plan to do afterward. Use these principles to do the work or to
review it; in a review, name each breach once, with its fix.

## Principles

1. **Decide why you are listening this time, before you start.** The same
   words call for different listening depending on the purpose, so name it. A
   risk of harm overrides the stated purpose.
2. **Keep empathy and evaluation apart.** Mixing them breeds conflict, though
   a brief acknowledgement before you judge is fine.
3. **When the purpose is empathy, hold back solutions.** Let them finish, even
   if it takes long.
4. **Show you followed by restating it in your own words.** Say back what they
   went through; a nod is not enough.
5. **Offer advice when the speaker invites it.** If no invitation comes, ask
   whether they want your view first.
6. **When the purpose is evaluation, hear the whole account before you
   judge.**

## Definitions

- **Empathy:** listening to understand how the speaker feels about their
  situation.
- **Evaluation:** listening in order to judge or solve.
