# experimentation

Principles for using a small, cheap, reversible test when thinking cannot
settle an uncertainty (`/experimentation`).

## Credit

- Principles 1 and 4 draw on Herminia Ibarra, *Working Identity: Unconventional
  Strategies for Reinventing Your Career*, Harvard Business School Press, 2003,
  for learning through action and trying several possible paths before
  committing.
- Principle 1 also draws on Amy C. Edmondson, “Strategies for Learning from
  Failure,” *Harvard Business Review* 89(4), 2011, for keeping tests at the
  smallest useful scale.
- Principle 2 draws on Saras D. Sarasvathy, “Causation and Effectuation: Toward
  a Theoretical Shift from Economic Inevitability to Entrepreneurial
  Contingency,” *Academy of Management Review* 26(2), 2001, for affordable loss
  rather than hoped-for return.
- The use of these ideas across domains, principles 3, 5, and 6, the remaining
  content of principles 1, 2, and 4, and the workflow, checklist, anti-patterns,
  and output are the author's own.
