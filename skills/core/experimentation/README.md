# experimentation

Learn through bounded action when thinking and existing evidence cannot settle
an uncertainty (`/experimentation`). `SKILL.md` gives the workflow;
`references/principles.md` holds the underlying principles.

## Credit

- Saras D. Sarasvathy, “Causation and Effectuation: Toward a Theoretical Shift
  from Economic Inevitability to Entrepreneurial Contingency,” *Academy of
  Management Review* 26(2), 2001
  ([article](https://doi.org/10.5465/amr.2001.4378020)).
- Amy C. Edmondson, “Strategies for Learning from Failure,” *Harvard Business
  Review* 89(4), 2011
  ([article](https://hbr.org/2011/04/strategies-for-learning-from-failure)).

Sarasvathy informs the affordable-downside rule; Edmondson informs small,
bounded failures used for learning. The generic extension, evidence rules,
reversibility test, and protections for affected people are this skill's own
synthesis.

This skill is an independent restatement. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any of these authors.
