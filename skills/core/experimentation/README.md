# experimentation

Principles for learning through bounded action when reasoning and existing
evidence cannot settle a decision-relevant uncertainty (`/experimentation`).

## Credit

- Principle 5 draws on Saras D. Sarasvathy, “Causation and Effectuation:
  Toward a Theoretical Shift from Economic Inevitability to Entrepreneurial
  Contingency,” *Academy of Management Review* 26(2), 2001, for affordable
  loss as the basis for limiting commitment.
- Principle 7 draws on Amy C. Edmondson, “Strategies for Learning from
  Failure,” *Harvard Business Review* 89(4), 2011, for small, bounded failures
  designed to produce learning.
- Principles 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 8, and the definitions, are author-created
  synthesis without external sources.
