# ownership

Principles for carrying an adopted outcome or binding obligation through to a
clear disposition instead of stopping at an assigned task (`/ownership`).

## Credit

- Principle 1 draws on Jocko Willink and Leif Babin, *Extreme Ownership: How
  U.S. Navy SEALs Lead and Win*, St. Martin's Press, 2015.
- Principle 3's rule to report mistakes early draws on Amy C. Edmondson, “Strategies for Learning from
  Failure,” *Harvard Business Review*, April 2011.
- Principles 2 and 4–7 are the author's own synthesis.

This skill is an independent restatement. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any of these authors.
