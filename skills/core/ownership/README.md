# ownership

Principles for carrying an adopted outcome or binding obligation through to a
clear disposition instead of stopping at an assigned task (`/ownership`).

## Credit

- Jocko Willink and Leif Babin, *Extreme Ownership: How U.S. Navy SEALs Lead
  and Win*, St. Martin's Press, 2015: principle 1 adapts the book's stance
  that a leader owns the whole result, and principle 3 its refusal to shift
  blame. The narrowing of ownership to agreed, assigned, or binding outcomes
  is the author's own.
- Amy C. Edmondson, “Strategies for Learning from Failure,” *Harvard Business
  Review*, April 2011: principle 3's rule to report mistakes early.
- The rest of principle 3, and principles 2 and 4–7, are the author's own.

This skill is an independent restatement. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any of these authors.
